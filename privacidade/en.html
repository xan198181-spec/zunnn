PRIVACY POLICY OF ZUNNN

v1.0 · 2026-10-10

Summary: Zunnn has no account, doesn't ask for your phone number or email, and has no server of its own. Your conversations stay on your device.

1. What stays only on your phone

  • Your conversations, contacts, conversation audio and identity keys are stored ENCRYPTED on the device (with a key protected by Android).
  • "My Day" videos and contact photos stay in the app's private folder, protected by Android (other apps can't read them), but WITHOUT additional encryption done by Zunnn.
  • None of this is sent to us. We have no access to any of it.

2. What leaves your phone and where it goes

  • Messages, contact requests, call notices and "My Day" notices travel end-to-end encrypted. Over the internet they pass through public "relays" (third-party servers of the Nostr protocol, such as nos.lol and relay.primal.net). They cannot read the content, but they can see that one key spoke to another and when. Over Bluetooth, only people nearby take part.
  • Voice and video calls and the "My Day" video go directly from one phone to the other (WebRTC). To find the path we use Google's public STUN servers, which see your IP address.
  • Weather, Moon, Sun, Sky: the app queries public third-party services (MET Norway, NOAA, NASA, USGS), sending your approximate location for the weather and forecast. They have their own policies.
  • Groups: each group message is sent, encrypted, to every member separately through the relays. Members see each other's names; only the admin sets the member list, name and cover photo.
  • Invites: when you open the app, it checks whether the text you copied contains a Zunnn invite and, if so, asks whether you want to add that person. What you copy is neither stored nor sent.
  • Temporary code: if you generate a code so someone can find you from afar, an encrypted notice with your key is published on the relays for 15 minutes, under a throwaway key. Only someone who knows the code can read it.
  • Location: used only on the device for the radar, compass, Sky and weather; it is only shared with a contact if you send it on purpose.
  • Purchases: Zunnn Plus is bought and billed through Google Play. Zunnn does not receive or store payment data; it only checks with Google that the purchase exists.

3. Permissions and why

  • Bluetooth: talk to nearby phones.
  • Location: compass, radar, weather, Sky.
  • Microphone: voice messages and calls.
  • Camera: read QR codes and video calls.
  • Notifications and background service: notify about messages and calls with the app closed (can be turned off in "Identity").
  • Photos/videos: only what you choose for "My Day".

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell data, we have no ads, we use no trackers, we don't read your conversations.

5. Your controls

  • Backup: you export your conversations to an encrypted file with a password of your own.
  • Delete: "Erase all my data" ("Identity") removes everything from the device. Messages already delivered to other people stay with them.
  • Block and report: in each conversation's menu.
  • If the app closes because of an error, it shows a report (app version, device model and the cause). The report stays on the device and only leaves if you tap Share.
  • Exporting the trail as GPX only happens when you tap the button; the file goes to the app you choose and contains the trail's GPS points.

6. Children

  • The app is not directed to children under 13.

7. Limits, honestly

  • Public relays can go down or drop messages. Calls and "My Day" may not connect on some mobile networks.
  • The security code helps prove that nobody is in the middle of the conversation; it only counts if you really compare it with the person.
